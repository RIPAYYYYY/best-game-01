Gloamgarden — a lanternkeeper's night watch: an original arena-survival game where ember wisps orbit you and burn back the glooms while you gather their fallen motes, choose a blessing at every level, and hold the garden until dawn.

## Run it

Serve the game locally with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.

Move with WASD, the arrow keys, or a held mouse or touch. Your wisps strike on their own — position is everything. Survive the four minutes to dawn; at 2:30 the Gloam Maw rises.
